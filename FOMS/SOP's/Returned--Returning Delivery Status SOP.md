When an order gets returned to the sender, either by the customer or a failed to deliver by the carrier, it will go into the RETURNING status and once it arrives at the warehouse it will go into the RETURNED status.
# Criteria
Tracking goes into `RETURNING` or `RETURNED` status.
# Next Steps
1. Have CS reach out to customer and gather info/offer reship or refund
2. Wait for response up to 48 hours
3. Reship if requested, otherwise refund
