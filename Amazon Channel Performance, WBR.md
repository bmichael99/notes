On Amazon, the TACoS acronym stands for ==**Total Advertising Cost of Sale**==. It is a vital metric that divides total ad spend by total revenue (combining both ad-driven and organic sales) to measure overall marketing efficiency. [[1](https://www.helium10.com/blog/what-is-amazon-tacos/), [2](https://forceget.com/blog/amazon-tacos-master-your-advertising-strategy/), [3](https://sellermetrics.app/amazon-acos-tacos/)]

How TACoS Works

- **Formula:** TACoS = (Total Ad Spend ÷ Total Revenue) × 100

- **Difference from ACoS:** Standard ACoS only divides ad spend by revenue generated directly from ads. TACoS looks at the big picture by including non-paid organic sales.

- **Health Indicator:** A stable or declining TACoS over time means your organic sales are growing nicely alongside your paid advertising.
