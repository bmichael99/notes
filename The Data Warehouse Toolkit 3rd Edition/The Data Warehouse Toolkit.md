Dimensional models should not be designed by focusing on predefined reports or analyses; the design should center on measurement processes.

We should be able to answer questions like, what type of customers are clicking on marketing pages, are they new customers, existing, etc.

Business processes are recognizable by their operational source.
- A business process is the operational activity that the organization performs -- events that generate or capture performance metrics.

When it’s time to tackle a DW/BI development project, start with a single business process matrix row because that minimizes the risk of signing up for an overly ambitious implementation.
